Privacy Policy

This policy explains, in simple terms, what information we use to run your Alibhai Points loyalty account and how you can contact us about it.

Who is responsible for your information?

The service is operated by Alibhai Group Versatile Trade Hub, at Rwagasore St, Mwanza, Tanzania. In this policy, “we” means that business and “you” means an Alibhai Points customer.

What information do we collect?

We use the information needed to operate the points program:

  • Your full name, phone number and city.
  • Your purchase amounts, points earned and points balance.
  • Your redemption requests and redemption history.
  • Login and account information handled by Supabase, our authentication and data hosting provider.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment card details through Alibhai Points.

Why do we use it, and where is it stored?

We use this information only to run the loyalty program: to identify your account, record qualifying purchases, calculate points, process redemptions and provide customer support. Your data is stored on Supabase servers. We do not sell your personal data.

Sera ya Faragha

Sera hii inaeleza kwa lugha rahisi taarifa tunazotumia kuendesha akaunti yako ya Alibhai Points na jinsi unavyoweza kuwasiliana nasi kuzihusu.

Nani anawajibika kwa taarifa zako?

Huduma hii inaendeshwa na Alibhai Group Versatile Trade Hub, katika Rwagasore St, Mwanza, Tanzania. Katika sera hii, “sisi” ni biashara hiyo na “wewe” ni mteja wa Alibhai Points.

Tunakusanya taarifa gani?

Tunatumia taarifa zinazohitajika kuendesha mpango wa pointi:

  • Jina lako kamili, namba ya simu na mji.
  • Kiasi cha manunuzi, pointi ulizopata na salio la pointi.
  • Maombi ya kutumia pointi na historia ya matumizi ya pointi.
  • Taarifa za kuingia na akaunti zinazoshughulikiwa na Supabase, mtoa huduma wetu wa uthibitishaji na uhifadhi wa data.

Kupitia Alibhai Points, hatukusanyi eneo ulipo, orodha ya watu wako, picha zako au taarifa za kadi za malipo.

Tunazitumia kwa nini, na zinahifadhiwa wapi?

Tunatumia taarifa hizi kuendesha mpango wa uaminifu pekee: kutambua akaunti yako, kurekodi manunuzi yanayostahili, kukokotoa pointi, kushughulikia matumizi ya pointi na kutoa msaada kwa wateja. Data yako huhifadhiwa kwenye seva za Supabase. Hatuuzi data yako binafsi.

Tunatunza taarifa kwa muda gani?

Tunatunza taarifa za akaunti na mpango kwa [MUDA WA KUTUNZA DATA], au kwa muda unaohitajika kuendesha mpango na kutimiza wajibu wa kisheria au uhasibu. Tunapaswa kutunza baadhi ya rekodi pale sheria inapohitaji; tunapunguza matumizi yake na kuzilinda.

Chaguo na maombi yako

Unaweza kuomba kuona au kusahihisha taarifa zako, au kuomba kufutwa kwa akaunti yako na taarifa binafsi zinazoweza kufutwa. Tutumie barua pepe au WhatsApp kupitia mawasiliano yaliyo hapa chini. Tunaweza kuthibitisha kuwa akaunti ni yako kabla ya kushughulikia ombi. Huenda tukalazimika kutunza baadhi ya rekodi kwa mujibu wa sheria.
