How to delete your account

You can ask us to close your Alibhai Points account. A member of our office team reviews and handles the request manually; submitting a request does not delete the account immediately.

Option 1: Request in the app

  1. Open the Alibhai Points app and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Choose Delete my account and submit the request.

Option 2: Contact us

Send an account deletion request by email or WhatsApp to [SUPPORT EMAIL / WHATSAPP CONTACT]. Include the phone number on your account so we can locate it. We will verify your identity before acting on the request.

What will be deleted?

After we verify and approve your request, we will close your loyalty account, remove your ability to sign in, and delete or anonymise personal account information where we are allowed to do so. Any unused points and ability to redeem points will end when the account is closed.

What may we need to keep?

We may retain purchase and transaction records needed for accounting, audit or other legal requirements for [RETENTION PERIOD IN DAYS] days. These records will be restricted and used only for those requirements. We do not keep information longer than necessary.

How long does it take?

We aim to review and complete a verified request within [PROCESSING TIME]. We will contact you if we need more information or if a legal record must be kept for longer.

Jinsi ya kufuta akaunti yako

Unaweza kuomba kufungwa kwa akaunti yako ya Alibhai Points. Mfanyakazi wa ofisi yetu hushughulikia ombi hilo mwenyewe baada ya kulipitia; kuwasilisha ombi hakufuti akaunti papo hapo.

Njia ya 1: Omba kupitia programu

  1. Fungua programu ya Alibhai Points na uingie kwenye akaunti.
  2. Fungua sehemu ya Wasifu.
  3. Chagua Futa akaunti yangu na utume ombi.

Njia ya 2: Wasiliana nasi

Tuma ombi la kufuta akaunti kwa barua pepe au WhatsApp kwa [MAWASILIANO YA BARUA PEPE / WHATSAPP]. Taja namba ya simu iliyosajiliwa kwenye akaunti ili tuweze kuitafuta. Tutathibitisha utambulisho wako kabla ya kuchukua hatua.

Nini kitafutwa?

Baada ya kuthibitisha na kuidhinisha ombi lako, tutafunga akaunti yako ya uaminifu, kuzuia kuingia tena, na kufuta au kutotambulisha taarifa binafsi za akaunti pale tunaporuhusiwa kufanya hivyo. Pointi ambazo hazijatumika na uwezo wa kutumia pointi utakoma akaunti itakapofungwa.

Ni taarifa gani tunaweza kulazimika kutunza?

Tunaweza kutunza rekodi za manunuzi na miamala zinazohitajika kwa uhasibu, ukaguzi au matakwa mengine ya kisheria kwa siku [IDADI YA SIKU ZA KUTUNZA REKODI]. Rekodi hizi zitadhibitiwa na kutumiwa kwa mahitaji hayo pekee. Hatutunzi taarifa kwa muda mrefu kuliko inavyohitajika.

Itachukua muda gani?

Tunalenga kupitia na kukamilisha ombi lililothibitishwa ndani ya [MUDA WA KUSHUGHULIKIA OMBI]. Tutawasiliana nawe tukihitaji taarifa zaidi au ikiwa rekodi ya kisheria itahitaji kutunzwa kwa muda mrefu zaidi.

Soma Sera yetu ya Faragha ili kujua jinsi tunavyoshughulikia taarifa zako, au wasiliana na [JINA RASMI LA BIASHARA] katika [ANUANI YA BIASHARA].
