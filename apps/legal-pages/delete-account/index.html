How to delete your account

You can ask us to close your Alibhai Points account. A member of our office team reviews and handles the request manually; submitting a request does not delete the account immediately.

Option 1: Request in the app

  1. Open the Alibhai Points app and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Choose Delete my account and submit the request.

Option 2: Contact us

Send an account deletion request by email or WhatsApp at +255 786 072 145. You can also call this number. Include the phone number on your account so we can locate it. We will verify your identity before acting on the request.

What will be deleted?

After we verify and approve your request, we will close your loyalty account, remove your ability to sign in, and delete or anonymise personal account information where we are allowed to do so. Any unused points and ability to redeem points will end when the account is closed.

What may we need to keep?

We may retain purchase and transaction records needed for accounting, auditing, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legal requirements for up to 365 days after account deletion, or longer where required by law. Access to these records will be restricted, and they will be used only for the relevant purposes. We do not keep personal information longer than necessary.

How long does it take?

We aim to review and complete verified requests within 30 days. We will contact you if we need more information or if legal recordkeeping requires us to retain records for longer.

Jinsi ya kufuta akaunti yako

Unaweza kuomba kufungwa kwa akaunti yako ya Alibhai Points. Mfanyakazi wa ofisi yetu hushughulikia ombi hilo mwenyewe baada ya kulipitia; kuwasilisha ombi hakufuti akaunti papo hapo.

Njia ya 1: Omba kupitia programu

  1. Fungua programu ya Alibhai Points na uingie kwenye akaunti.
  2. Fungua sehemu ya Wasifu.
  3. Chagua Futa akaunti yangu na utume ombi.

Njia ya 2: Wasiliana nasi

Tuma ombi la kufuta akaunti kwa barua pepe au WhatsApp kupitia +255 786 072 145. Unaweza pia kupiga simu kwenye namba hii. Taja namba ya simu iliyosajiliwa kwenye akaunti ili tuweze kuitafuta. Tutathibitisha utambulisho wako kabla ya kuchukua hatua.

Nini kitafutwa?

Baada ya kuthibitisha na kuidhinisha ombi lako, tutafunga akaunti yako ya uaminifu, kuzuia kuingia tena, na kufuta au kutotambulisha taarifa binafsi za akaunti pale tunaporuhusiwa kufanya hivyo. Pointi ambazo hazijatumika na uwezo wa kutumia pointi utakoma akaunti itakapofungwa.

Ni taarifa gani tunaweza kulazimika kutunza?

Tunaweza kutunza rekodi za manunuzi na miamala zinazohitajika kwa uhasibu, ukaguzi, kuzuia udanganyifu, kutatua migogoro au kutimiza matakwa mengine ya kisheria kwa muda wa hadi siku 365 baada ya akaunti kufutwa, au kwa muda mrefu zaidi pale sheria inapohitaji. Upatikanaji wa rekodi hizi utadhibitiwa, na zitatumiwa kwa madhumuni husika pekee. Hatutunzi taarifa binafsi kwa muda mrefu kuliko inavyohitajika.

Itachukua muda gani?

Tunakusudia kupitia na kukamilisha maombi yaliyothibitishwa ndani ya siku 30. Tutawasiliana nawe ikiwa tutahitaji taarifa zaidi au ikiwa utunzaji wa rekodi za kisheria unatuhitaji kutunza rekodi kwa muda mrefu zaidi.
